What we collect, and why

  • When you email us at hello@vellfire.ai: your name, email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply to you and, if you go ahead, to set up your VellFire build and send your licence key. We never sell it or share it.   • Website visits: we count anonymous page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics (no cookies, no personal data). It records things like the page viewed, the referring site and the country, never who you are. Our legal basis is legitimate interests (knowing which pages people use).
  • Product news: we only send it if you ask us to, and you can stop it at any time. Our legal basis is your consent.

The VellFire.Ai program itself runs on your PC. Your trades, keys and settings stay on your computer and are not sent to us. Market data flows directly between your PC and your broker; we don't receive it.

Who sees it

We never sell your data. We do not share it for advertising. Your emails are kept in our business email account. Your trading data stays on your PC.

How long we keep it

Emails and client records: kept until you ask us to delete them, or 2 years after your last contact with us, whichever comes first (licence and company tax records may be kept longer where the law requires). Product news: sent only if you asked for it, until you ask us to stop. If you ask us to delete your data, we do so unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You can ask us to: give you a copy of your data; correct it; delete it; limit or object to how we use it; move it to another service; and withdraw consent to product updates at any time. Email hello@vellfire.ai. We aim to reply within one month. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113).
