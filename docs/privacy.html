We never sell your data. We do not share it for advertising. It is stored with our service provider Cloudflare, which processes it for us under its data-processing terms, and may be sent to us as an email notification.

Sign-up and download records: kept until you ask us to delete them, or 2 years after your last contact with us, whichever comes first (licence and company tax records may be kept longer where the law requires). Product-update emails: sent only if you ticked "Email me product updates", until you unsubscribe or ask us to stop. Hashed IP data (used only to stop spam): kept no longer than the sign-up record. If you ask us to delete your data, we do so unless the law requires us to keep it.
