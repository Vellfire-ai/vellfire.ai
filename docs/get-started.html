Get started

Ten minutes to your first chart.

Most people are looking at their first chart within ten minutes. You need a Windows 10 or 11 PC and a data connection (or demo data to look around).

iVellFire is trading software, not financial advice. It never tells you what to buy or sell. Trading carries a risk of loss, and you can lose more than you expect. Read the risk warning

1Download and install

  1. Click Download for Windows and save VellFire_Basic_Setup.exe.
  2. Your browser may ask whether to keep the file. Choose Keep.
  3. Run the installer. You don't need admin rights or any other software.

⬇ Download for Windows

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC"

VellFire is new and not yet code-signed, so Windows doesn't recognise it yet. This message is normal for new apps.

  1. Click More info.
  2. Check that the publisher line names the VellFire app, then click Run anyway.

You'll only see this once. When code signing is in place, the message goes away.

Portable version (no install): VellFire_Basic_Portable.exe runs from any folder. Download the portable version. If Smart App Control blocks it, use the run-from-folder copy: unzip it and double-click Start_VellFire_Basic.bat.

2First run

  1. Read and accept the risk warning and terms.
  2. Set your risk per trade and daily loss limit. You can change these outside market hours.
  3. Choose your data: Interactive Brokers, Alpaca, or demo data. Intraday charts and paper trading: Interactive Brokers (or demo data) in the beta; Alpaca free covers daily charts and research now, intraday coming in November.

3aConnect Interactive Brokers

You need: an Interactive Brokers account and Trader Workstation (TWS) or IB Gateway installed.

  1. Open TWS or IB Gateway and log in.
  2. In TWS go to File › Global Configuration › API › Settings. Tick Enable ActiveX and Socket Clients. Leave Read-Only API ticked.
  3. Note the port:
    ProgramLivePaper
    TWS74967497
    IB Gateway40014002
  4. In VellFire go to Setup › Data › Interactive Brokers, enter the port and click Connect. The status light in the top bar turns green.
  5. Market data: live streaming prices need market data subscriptions on your IBKR account (Client Portal › Settings › Market Data Subscriptions): the US Securities Snapshot and Futures Value Bundle plus the US Equity and Options Add-On Streaming Bundle. Level 2 needs NASDAQ TotalView-OpenView (and its EDS feed for third-party platforms). IBKR sets the prices; see IBKR's market data page for current fees.

Note: VellFire keeps TWS or IB Gateway in read-only mode. In the beta, paper trades are filled by VellFire's simulator using real prices.

3bConnect Alpaca, free

You need: a free Alpaca account.

  1. Sign up at alpaca.markets and open your dashboard.
  2. Choose your paper account and click Generate New Keys under API Keys.
  3. Copy the Key ID and Secret Key.
  4. In VellFire go to Setup › Data › Alpaca, paste both and click Connect.
  5. What a free Alpaca account gives you: live prices from one exchange (IEX), full-market data 15 minutes delayed, and over 7 years of history. A paid Alpaca account adds all exchanges in real time.

Your keys are stored on your PC only. Intraday charts and paper trading: Interactive Brokers (or demo data) in the beta; Alpaca free covers daily charts and research now, intraday coming in November.

4Your first session

  • Open Chartroom, type a ticker in the command bar, and try "add 20 EMA" or "5 minute".
  • Build a watchlist and save a layout.
  • In Trade, set a stop and size an order by risk. Check the preview, then send the paper order.
  • After the close, open Journal to review the trade.

?If something isn't working

  • Charts empty or not updating? Check that TWS or IB Gateway is running and logged in, or that your Alpaca keys are entered. Look at the status light in the top bar.
  • Still stuck? Click Get help in VellFire (see Help).
