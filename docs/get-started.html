How setup works

Set up one-to-one.

VellFire is set up with you, on your own PC. You need a Windows 10 or 11 PC, your own broker and data, and your own Claude account.

iVellFire is trading software, not financial advice. It never tells you what to buy or sell. Trading carries a risk of loss, and you can lose more than you expect. Read the risk warning

1The steps

  1. Tell us how you trade. By email, you tell us the stocks, the setups, your entries and exits, and the rules you want held.
  2. Installed on your PC. We install VellFire on your PC and connect your own broker and data.
  3. Connect your Claude. You link your own Claude account, so you can change charts, scans, your journal and setups just by asking. Your risk limits stay locked.
  4. Licensed to you. Your copy runs on a named licence key for you alone.

For more details, get in touch: hello@vellfire.ai

2aConnect Interactive Brokers

You need: your own Interactive Brokers account and Trader Workstation (TWS) or IB Gateway on your PC. We do this with you during setup.

  1. Open TWS or IB Gateway and log in.
  2. In TWS go to File › Global Configuration › API › Settings. Tick Enable ActiveX and Socket Clients. Leave Read-Only API ticked.
  3. Note the port:
    ProgramLivePaper
    TWS74967497
    IB Gateway40014002
  4. In VellFire go to Setup › Data › Interactive Brokers, enter the port and click Connect. The status light in the top bar turns green.
  5. Market data: live streaming prices and Level 2 need the matching market data subscriptions on your own IBKR account (Client Portal › Settings › Market Data Subscriptions). Those are set and charged by IBKR; see IBKR's market data page for current details.

Note: VellFire keeps TWS or IB Gateway in read-only mode. Paper trades are filled by VellFire's simulator using real prices.

2bConnect Alpaca

You need: your own Alpaca account.

  1. Sign in at alpaca.markets and open your dashboard.
  2. Choose your paper account and click Generate New Keys under API Keys.
  3. Copy the Key ID and Secret Key.
  4. In VellFire go to Setup › Data › Alpaca, paste both and click Connect.

Your keys are stored on your PC only.

?If something isn't working

  • Charts empty or not updating? Check that TWS or IB Gateway is running and logged in, or that your Alpaca keys are entered. Look at the status light in the top bar.
  • Still stuck? Click Get help in VellFire (see Help).
