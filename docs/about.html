About VellFire

Built for the hard part of trading

iVellFire is trading software, not financial advice. It never tells you what to buy or sell. Trading carries a risk of loss, and you can lose more than you expect. Read the risk warning

Finding ideas is rarely the hard part of trading. Sticking to your plan when the market moves against you is.

VellFire started as a tool for one trader to enforce his own rules: a stop on every order, size from the stop, and a daily limit that holds. It has grown into a full desk for self-directed traders, with charts, research, order entry, a journal and tax records, all built around those rules.

What we believe

  • Your rules, not ours. VellFire never tells you what to buy. It shows whether a trade fits the rules you set.
  • Your data, on your PC. We don't need your trades to run a business.
  • Plain and simple. Free for early users. No data mark-up.
  • Honest status. We're in beta and say so.
