About

Built by a trader.
For the hard part.

The hard part of trading was never finding ideas. It was following the plan when the screen was moving.

VellFire.Ai started as one trader's own desk: a tool that would hold him to the rules he had already written. A stop on every order. Size from the stop. A daily limit that could not be talked out of in the heat of the session. Rules checked against every stock, with the numbers shown, so the decision was clear and still his own.

That desk grew into a full workstation: charts, scanner, Montage and ladder, backtester, journal, a market page and a coach that reads the rulebook. It runs on your PC, with your broker, your data and your Claude key. There are no VellFire servers and no account, because the tool was built for one person's privacy first.

Now it's shared, in beta, with the same rule at its centre: VellFire never tells you what to buy. It tells you whether a stock fits your rules. Your decision.
