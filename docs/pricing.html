What you bring

A Windows PC

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

A data connection

Your Interactive Brokers account, or a free Alpaca account. Intraday charts and paper trading: Interactive Brokers (or demo data) in the beta; Alpaca free covers daily charts and research now, intraday coming in November.

Nothing else

Market data costs, if any, are paid to your broker or Alpaca, not to us. VellFire doesn't sell or mark up data. Data costs explained

What happens after the Pro trial

  1. Days 1–14: every Pro feature is unlocked. The trial starts after the beta ends.
  2. Before day 14, we email you a reminder. [CONFIRM]
  3. If you subscribe, nothing changes and you keep working.
  4. If you don't, you stay on Free: the Pro features lock, and Chart room, Research, Journal and paper trading keep working. Your data stays on your PC. Nothing is deleted.

Cancel and refunds

  • Cancel any time. Your plan runs to the end of the month you've paid for.
  • No refund for a month already paid.
  • UK and EU customers: your statutory rights are not affected. [CONFIRM] wording with Terms

Pricing questions

Is Free really free?

Yes. Chart room, Research, Journal and paper trading are free to use, with no card and no time limit. You pay only when you want Pro, which adds live trading.

Will the Advanced package cost extra?

It will be an optional add-on, with details in Spring 2027.

Can I use it on two PCs?

Yes, for one person on the PCs you trade from. It can't be shared.
