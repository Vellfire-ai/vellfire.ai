What happens after the trial

  1. Days 1–14: everything is unlocked.
  2. Before day 14, we email you a reminder. [CONFIRM]
  3. If you subscribe, nothing changes and you keep working.
  4. If you don't, the trading tabs lock. Your journal, settings and data stay on your PC. You can still view and export your journal and close paper trades. Nothing is deleted.

Cancel and refunds
