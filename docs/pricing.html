What you bring

VellFire runs on your own accounts, at each provider's own price. We add nothing on top.

Your broker

Interactive Brokers (paper account) or an IG demo account. VellFire reads prices from your broker; paper fills are VellFire's own. Any market-data subscriptions are yours, in your broker account.

Your data keys

Free data options are built in. Add keys for more history or options data if you want them; each key is stored only on your PC and only sent to the address you typed for it.

Your Claude key

Every AI feature uses your own key, pay-as-you-go. Daily use is usually about £5-15 a month; long coach chats and course builds cost the most. Set a monthly cap inside VellFire.

Questions

What happens when the trial ends?

You get a licence key by email after sign-up and paste it into Setup. Until then your data (view, export, back up, delete), your risk controls, Setup, the Guide and closing paper trades always stay available. Nothing is deleted.

Is there a cheaper plan?

No. One plan, every feature. The trial is the way to find out if it earns its place on your desk.

Why is it in beta?

VellFire is a working tool used daily by its builder, and it is still growing fast. Beta means you get everything now and the roadmap as it ships, and we ask for your feedback.

Does it place real orders?

Paper trading is the default and what you get today. Nothing is sent to a live account.

Does VellFire tell me what to buy?

No. It checks stocks against the rules you wrote and tells you "Fits your rules" or "Doesn't fit your rules", with the numbers. The decision is always yours. It is not financial advice.

Can I run it on two PCs?

The licence is per person. Use it on the PCs you trade from; it isn't for sharing or reselling.
