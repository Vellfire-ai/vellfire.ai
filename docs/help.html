Getting started

What do I need to run VellFire?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC, an internet connection, and a market data source: your Interactive Brokers account or a free Alpaca account. VellFire runs on your own PC and keeps your data there.

Does it work on a Mac?

Not at the moment. VellFire Basic is a Windows app. [CONFIRM]

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

That message appears for new apps that Windows hasn't seen many times yet. VellFire isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. You'll only see it once.

My browser says the download "isn't commonly downloaded".

This happens for the same reason. Choose Keep (or Keep anyway).

How do I get updates?

VellFire updates itself outside US market hours. Your settings, layouts and journal are kept.

Data and brokers

Where do the prices come from?

From your own data connection. VellFire doesn't sell market data; you connect the account you already have. See Connect Interactive Brokers or Connect Alpaca.

What will market data cost me?

VellFire doesn't charge for data. With Alpaca, the free plan gives one exchange live, the full market 15 minutes delayed, and years of history. With Interactive Brokers, you pay IBKR for the subscriptions you choose. Level 2 needs its own subscription. Check current prices with your provider. Intraday charts and paper trading: Interactive Brokers (or demo data) in the beta; Alpaca free covers daily charts and research now, intraday coming in November.

Which brokers does VellFire work with?

Interactive Brokers (for data, through TWS or IB Gateway) and Alpaca (free data key). Intraday charts and paper trading: Interactive Brokers (or demo data) in the beta; Alpaca free covers daily charts and research now, intraday coming in November. VellFire is not affiliated with either.

Why are my charts empty or not updating?

Usually the data connection. Check that Trader Workstation or IB Gateway is running and logged in, or that your Alpaca keys are entered. The status light in the top bar shows the connection.

Interactive Brokers questions

Do I need to log in to both IBKR and VellFire?

Yes. Keep TWS or IB Gateway running and logged in; VellFire connects to it on your PC. VellFire never sees your IBKR password.

A position I closed in TWS or the IBKR app still shows in VellFire. What do I do?

Click Refresh positions in Trade (or press F5). VellFire reads positions and orders from IBKR, so IBKR is always the source of truth. If it still shows, restart TWS/IB Gateway, then reconnect.

Why doesn't buying power or equity match IBKR?

VellFire shows IBKR's own account figures, refreshed every few seconds. Small gaps come from timing or currency (VellFire shows your base currency). Click Refresh account. Your risk per trade is set by you in Risk, not by buying power.

How do I cancel stuck or invalid orders?

Use Cancel all in Trade, or cancel in TWS. VellFire reflects what IBKR holds.

"Could not create SSL/TLS secure channel" or connection refused

Check TWS/IB Gateway is running, the API is enabled, and the port in VellFire matches (see the port table). Make sure only one program uses the same client ID. Firewall or antivirus can block local connections: allow VellFire.

Can I trade overnight or pre-market?

Pre-market and after-hours orders work where IBKR allows them (tick "Outside regular hours" on the order). Overnight trading is an IBKR permission you request in Client Portal. [CONFIRM] order flag supported in Basic

How do I convert currency?

In IBKR (Client Portal or TWS), not in VellFire. US stocks need USD in your account or IBKR's auto-conversion.

Trading

Can VellFire place real trades?

Paper trading in the beta; live trading with your own IBKR account from launch. In the beta, real prices with simulated fills.

Does VellFire tell me what to buy?

No. It shows whether a trade fits the rules you set. It is not financial advice, and every decision is yours.

Can I turn off the daily loss limit during the day?

No. During market hours you can tighten your limits but not loosen them. That's deliberate. You can change them after the close.

Is the tax export tax advice?

No. It's a record of your trades, matched the way UK and US rules describe, to help you or your accountant. Check it before you file.

Privacy

Is my data private?

Yes. Your journal, settings and keys stay on your PC. VellFire never sends your trades or keys to us. When you ask for help, you see exactly what's in the support file before it's sent.

Do I need a VellFire account?

No login account. We only hold the email address your licence key is sent to. [CONFIRM]
